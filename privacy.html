Privacy Policy

Kira personal finance application

About Privacy Policy Terms

Effective: 6 October 2026

Overview

Kira is a personal finance application. This policy explains the information Kira processes, why it is used, and the controls available to you.

Information you provide

Kira may store account information and the finance records you choose to enter, including transactions, transfers, account and credit-card details, budgets, recurring items, goals, categories, tags, Split Bills, payment records, notes, and receipt files.

Google sign-in data

If you choose Continue with Google, Kira uses Google only for authentication. Kira may receive basic account information made available through the sign-in flow, such as your Google account identifier, name, email address, and profile image.

Kira uses this Google user data to create or authenticate your Kira account and to display basic account identity inside the app. Kira does not use Google sign-in to read Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, contacts, or other Google service content.

How information is used

Information is used to provide Kira's finance features, keep your account signed in, calculate balances and reports, manage Split Bills and recurring records, support data export and deletion, maintain security, and troubleshoot the service.

Service providers

Kira relies on infrastructure providers to operate the application. Supabase provides authentication, database and related backend services, while Vercel provides application hosting and delivery. These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services.

Sharing and selling

Kira does not sell Google sign-in data. Information may be processed by service providers that operate Kira, or disclosed when required to protect users, maintain the service, or comply with applicable law.

Security and account isolation

Kira uses authenticated accounts and account-level access controls intended to keep one user's finance records separate from another user's records. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so users should also protect their sign-in credentials and devices.

Export and deletion

Signed-in users can use Kira's Settings to export a copy of supported finance records. Kira also provides an account deletion flow for permanently deleting the Kira account and associated supported records.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Kira's features or data practices change. The effective date on this page will be updated when material revisions are published.
