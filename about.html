What Kira does

Kira helps you organise day-to-day personal finances in one place. You can record income and expenses, manage accounts and credit cards, plan budgets, track recurring payments and goals, review reports, and manage Split Bills with other people.

Sign in

You can sign in with email and password or, where enabled, with Google. Google sign-in is used to authenticate your Kira account; Kira does not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendar, or other Google service content as part of the standard sign-in flow.

Your data stays tied to your account

Kira uses account-level access controls so signed-in users access their own finance records. Settings also include options to export your Kira data and request permanent account deletion.
